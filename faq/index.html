Frequently Asked Questions

Heating, Cooling and Furnace Filters
How Often Should I Change My Furnace Filter?

Every 1 to 3 months for standard 1-inch filters, more often with pets. Most homes go far longer, which strains the blower and raises energy bills. Every plan includes Furnace Filters changed at each visit.

How Often Does a Furnace Need a Tune-Up?

Once a year, ideally in the fall before heating season. Every plan includes a fall furnace tune-up for every furnace.

How Often Should My AC Be Serviced?

Once a year in spring, before the first hot week. Every plan includes a spring AC tune-up for every AC unit.

Is Skipped Maintenance Really That Costly?

Often, yes. A clogged filter, a blocked dryer vent or an overflowing gutter can turn a small fix into a repair or a denied warranty claim. A plan keeps a dated record of every visit.

Safety Checks
How Often Should Smoke Detector Batteries Be Changed?

Test monthly and replace batteries at least once a year, unless the unit has a sealed 10-year battery. Every plan tests smoke and CO detectors at each visit.

When Should Smoke and CO Detectors Be Replaced?

Smoke alarms at 10 years. CO alarms typically at 5 to 7 years, depending on the model; check the date on the back. We flag expired units during visits.

How Risky Is a Clogged Dryer Vent?

Lint buildup is a leading cause of home dryer fires, and a clogged vent also makes drying slower. Clean it at least once a year. Every plan includes dryer vent cleaning.

Water, Gutters and Michigan Winters
How Often Should Gutters Be Cleaned?

At least twice a year, spring and fall, and more under heavy trees. Plus and Complete include gutter and downspout cleaning, spring and fall.

Should I Flush My Water Heater?

Yes, about once a year, to clear sediment that shortens tank life. Tankless units need descaling. Every plan includes a water heater flush or tankless descale.

How Often Should I Test My Sump Pump?

Every few months and before spring thaw: pour water into the pit and make sure it starts and drains. Plus and Complete service your sump pump.

How Do I Prep My Home for a Michigan Winter?

Tune the furnace, clean gutters, shut off and drain outside spigots, blow out irrigation and check weatherstripping. Plus covers pre-winter weatherproofing; Complete adds ice dam watch.

What Is the Mold and Moisture Scan?

A pinless moisture meter and thermal camera check under bathrooms, windows, basement walls and sinks. It checks for moisture and signs of mold, and flags damp spots where mold can grow. It is not a lab mold test. Readings and photos go to your Home Record. It's in every plan.
